# Privacy Policy for Blueprints Pro

**Last Updated: January 2025**

## Introduction

Blueprints Pro ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our offline-first blueprint annotation application.

## Data Collection

### What We Collect

The App operates **entirely offline** and does NOT collect, transmit, or store any user data on external servers. All data remains on your device.

The following data is stored **locally on your device only**:

- Blueprint images and annotations you create
- Project data and organizational information
- Layer configurations and settings
- App preferences and theme settings
- Local notifications you schedule

### What We DO NOT Collect

- Personal identification information
- Location data
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Crash reports (unless you explicitly opt-in through device settings)
- Device information
- Network activity

## Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device using:
- Encrypted SQLite databases via WatermelonDB
- Device file system for images and exports
- iOS/Android system preferences for settings

## Data Sharing

**We do not share any data** because we do not have access to your data. All data remains on your device at all times.

## Third-Party Services

### In-App Purchases

The App uses Apple's In-App Purchase system for premium features. Apple may collect information related to your purchases according to their privacy policy. We do not receive any personal information from these transactions beyond:
- Purchase confirmation
- Product ID purchased
- Transaction receipt (for verification only)

This information is processed locally on your device and never transmitted to our servers.

### Local Notifications

The App uses iOS's local notification system. Notifications are scheduled and displayed entirely on your device. No notification data is transmitted externally.

## Data Export

You have full control over your data through the App's export functionality:
- Export to PDF format
- Export to Markdown format
- Export to JSON format

When you export data, you explicitly choose where to save or share the files. The App does not automatically send exported files anywhere.

## Data Deletion

You can delete all app data by:
1. Deleting individual blueprints within the App
2. Uninstalling the App from your device

Uninstalling the App will permanently delete all locally stored data from your device.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Since all data is stored locally and we do not collect any data externally, children can safely use the App under parental supervision.

## Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:
- Encrypted local database storage
- Secure file system access
- iOS/Android system-level security

Since all data remains on your device, your data security primarily depends on your device's security settings (passcode, Face ID, etc.).

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Posting the new Privacy Policy in the App
- Updating the "Last Updated" date
- Notifying you through the App (for significant changes)

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access all your data within the App
- Export your data in multiple formats
- Delete your data at any time
- Control notification permissions through device settings

## iOS Permissions

The App may request the following iOS permissions:
- **Photos/Camera**: To import blueprint images (optional)
- **Notifications**: To schedule reminders (optional)
- **Files**: To export blueprints (optional)

All permissions are optional and the App will function without them, with reduced functionality.

## Compliance

This Privacy Policy complies with:
- Apple App Store Review Guidelines
- iOS Privacy Requirements
- COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) principles

## Data Retention

Since all data is stored locally on your device:
- Data is retained until you explicitly delete it
- Data is automatically deleted when you uninstall the App
- No data retention policies apply to external servers (as we don't use any)

## Offline Operation

The App is designed to work completely offline:
- No internet connection required
- No data transmitted over the network
- No cloud storage or synchronization
- Complete privacy by design
